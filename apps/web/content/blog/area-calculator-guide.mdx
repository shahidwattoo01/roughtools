Area is the two-dimensional space enclosed within a shape, measured in square units (square feet, square meters, square inches, etc.). For a rectangle: Area = Length × Width. For a circle: Area = π × radius². For a triangle: Area = (base × height) / 2. A room that is 14 feet long and 12 feet wide has an area of 168 square feet.

Area calculations arise in home improvement (flooring, paint, tile), landscaping (sod, mulch, fertilizer), real estate (lot size, livable space), construction, fabric and crafts, and geometry problems. Understanding the area formula for each shape — and when to apply which — is a core practical math skill.

## Area Formulas for Common Shapes

| Shape | Formula | Variables |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Rectangle | A = l × w | l = length, w = width |
| Square | A = s² | s = side length |
| Circle | A = π × r² | r = radius |
| Triangle | A = (b × h) / 2 | b = base, h = height |
| Trapezoid | A = (a + b) / 2 × h | a, b = parallel sides, h = height |
| Parallelogram | A = b × h | b = base, h = perpendicular height |
| Ellipse | A = π × a × b | a = semi-major axis, b = semi-minor axis |
| Regular Hexagon | A = (3√3 / 2) × s² | s = side length |

## Practical Area Calculation Guide

**How much paint do you need?**
Measure walls: (perimeter × wall height) − (door and window area). One gallon of paint typically covers 350–400 sq ft. Add 10–15% for second coat or overlap.

**How much flooring do you need?**
Calculate room area, then add 5–10% for cuts and waste. For diagonal tile installation, add 15%. For hardwood with varying plank lengths, add 10–15%.

**How much fertilizer/seed for a lawn?**
Calculate total lawn area (usually a combination of rectangles and irregular shapes). Most fertilizers specify coverage in square feet (e.g., 1 bag covers 5,000 sq ft).

**How much tile for a backsplash?**
Measure the backsplash area (width × height, minus stove/vent hood space). Standard tile coverage: tiles are priced per square foot. Add 10% for cuts and breakage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I calculate area of an irregular shape?**
Divide the irregular shape into regular shapes (rectangles, triangles) that you can measure individually. Calculate the area of each regular shape using its formula. Add all the areas together (and subtract any cutouts or holes). For very irregular plots, the Shoelace Formula uses GPS coordinates of boundary points to calculate exact area.

**What is the difference between perimeter and area?**
Area measures the space inside a shape (in square units). Perimeter measures the total length of the boundary of a shape (in linear units). A rectangular room with length 12 ft and width 10 ft has area = 120 sq ft and perimeter = 2(12+10) = 44 ft. You need area to calculate flooring; you need perimeter to calculate baseboards or fencing.

**How do I convert square feet to square meters?**
1 square foot = 0.0929 square meters. Or: 1 square meter = 10.764 square feet. To convert: sq ft × 0.0929 = sq meters; sq meters × 10.764 = sq ft. A 2,000 square foot home = 2,000 × 0.0929 = 185.8 square meters.

**How much is 1 acre in square feet?**
1 acre = 43,560 square feet. It is approximately the area of a square with sides of 208.7 feet. Common area reference points: a football field (including end zones) is approximately 1.3 acres (57,600 sq ft); a typical suburban lot is 0.1–0.25 acres; a city block averages 2–5 acres depending on the city.

**How do I find the area of a curved or irregular boundary using GPS coordinates?**
The Shoelace Formula (also called Gauss's area formula) computes the area of a polygon from the coordinates of its vertices: Area = (1/2)|Σ(x_i × y_{i+1} − x_{i+1} × y_i)|. Modern tools like Google Earth, land surveying software, and GPS apps apply this automatically when you trace a boundary. For rough approximations of irregular outdoor areas, divide into roughly rectangular or triangular sections and sum the individual areas.
