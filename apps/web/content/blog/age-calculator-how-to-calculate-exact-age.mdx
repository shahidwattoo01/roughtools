## How to Calculate Your Exact Age

Your age in years is simply the difference between the current year and your birth year, minus one if your birthday hasn't occurred yet this year. But calculating exact age in months and days requires accounting for varying month lengths and leap years.

## What Is a Leap Year?

A leap year has 366 days instead of 365, with February 29 added. Leap years occur every 4 years, except for years divisible by 100 (not leap years), except for years divisible by 400 (are leap years). So 2000 was a leap year; 1900 was not.

If you were born on February 29, most countries celebrate your birthday on either February 28 or March 1 in non-leap years. Legally, most jurisdictions treat March 1 as your birthday in non-leap years.
