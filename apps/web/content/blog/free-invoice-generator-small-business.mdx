## Why Professional Invoices Matter

A professional invoice does more than request payment — it establishes trust, sets clear payment terms, and creates a paper trail for taxes. Research shows freelancers who use professional invoices get paid an average of 11 days faster than those using informal requests.

## What Every Invoice Must Include

- **Invoice number** — Sequential, unique identifier for your records
- **Issue date and due date** — Typically Net 15, Net 30, or Net 60
- **Your business name and contact details**
- **Client name and address**
- **Itemized list of services or products**
- **Subtotal, taxes (if applicable), and total**
- **Payment method instructions**

## Payment Terms Explained

| Term | Meaning | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Due on receipt | Pay immediately | Small one-time jobs |
| Net 15 | Pay within 15 days | Freelancers, small agencies |
| Net 30 | Pay within 30 days | Standard business invoicing |
| Net 60 | Pay within 60 days | Large enterprises |
| 2/10 Net 30 | 2% discount if paid in 10 days | Incentivizing early payment |

## Invoice vs. Receipt vs. Quote

**Quote/Estimate** — Sent before work begins. Estimates cost, not binding.

**Invoice** — Sent after work is complete (or at milestones). Requests payment. Legally binding when accepted.

**Receipt** — Confirmation that payment was received. Issued after payment.
