## What Is a Length Converter?

A length converter is a tool that instantly translates a measurement expressed in one unit of length into its equivalents in other units — for example, entering 5 feet 10 inches and getting back 1.778 meters, 177.8 centimeters, 70 inches, and 1.944 yards all at once.

Length and distance units fall into two systems: the metric system (millimeters, centimeters, meters, kilometers) used by most of the world, and the imperial/US customary system (inches, feet, yards, miles) used primarily in the United States. Conversions between them arise constantly in everyday life — product dimensions, travel distances, height measurements, construction plans, and athletic race distances.

The foundational conversion is exact by international agreement: 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters exactly. All other conversions derive from this: 1 foot = 30.48 cm exactly; 1 yard = 91.44 cm exactly; 1 mile = 1,609.344 meters exactly. For quick mental math: 1 meter ≈ 3.3 feet; 1 km ≈ 0.6 miles; 1 mile ≈ 1.6 km.

The converter is particularly useful for height conversion (feet/inches to centimeters is extremely common when filling international forms), screen and product dimensions (US manufacturers list in inches; European specs list in centimeters), and running distances (5K = 3.1 miles; 10K = 6.2 miles; marathon = 26.2 miles = 42.2 km).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the difference between a US mile and a nautical mile?**
A US (land/statute) mile = 5,280 feet = 1.609 km. A nautical mile = 1,852 meters = 1.151 statute miles. The nautical mile is based on the Earth's circumference — one nautical mile equals one arc-minute of latitude. It is used in aviation and maritime navigation because speed in knots (nautical miles per hour) relates directly to navigation by latitude/longitude.

**What is the exact relationship between inches and centimeters?**
By international agreement (1959), 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters exactly. This is a defined conversion, not a measured approximation — all subsequent conversions (feet, yards, miles) derive from this exact value. Therefore: 1 foot = 30.48 cm exactly; 1 yard = 91.44 cm exactly; 1 mile = 1,609.344 meters exactly.

**How do I convert feet and inches to meters?**
Convert everything to inches first: total inches = (feet × 12) + remaining inches. Then multiply by 0.0254 (since 1 inch = 0.0254 meters exactly). Example: 5 feet 9 inches = (5×12) + 9 = 69 inches × 0.0254 = 1.7526 meters.

**What is a millimeter vs. a micrometer vs. a nanometer?**
1 millimeter (mm) = 0.001 meters = 1/25.4 inch. 1 micrometer (μm) = 0.000001 meters = 1/1000 of a mm. 1 nanometer (nm) = 0.000000001 meters = 1/1000 of a μm. Context: human hair is 50–100 μm thick; red blood cells are 6–8 μm; visible light wavelengths are 380–700 nm; computer chip features are now below 3 nm.

**What length units are used in astronomy?**
Astronomical distances are too large for kilometers or miles. Astronomers use: Astronomical Unit (AU) = average Earth-Sun distance ≈ 150 million km. Light-year (ly) = distance light travels in one year ≈ 9.46 trillion km. Parsec (pc) = 3.26 light-years, defined by parallax measurement. Kiloparsec (kpc), megaparsec (Mpc) for galaxy-scale distances. The nearest star (Proxima Centauri) is 4.24 light-years away; the observable universe is about 93 billion light-years in diameter.
