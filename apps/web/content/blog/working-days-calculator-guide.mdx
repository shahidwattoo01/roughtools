To count business days between two dates, exclude Saturdays and Sundays (and optionally holidays). From Monday June 15 to Friday June 26, 2026 (12 calendar days): removing 2 weekends (4 days) = **8 business days**. To add 30 business days to June 15, 2026: count forward 30 Mon–Fri days = August 25, 2026 (approximately 6 weeks).

## How Business Day Calculation Works

**Counting business days between two dates:**
```
Business Days = Calendar Days - (Saturdays + Sundays in range) - Holidays
```

For each week in the range: 5 business days. For partial weeks at start and end, count only weekdays in those partial weeks.

**Adding N business days to a date:**
Start on the start date. Count forward only on Mon–Fri. Skip Sat, Sun, and any holidays. Count N weekday steps.

**Example — June 14, 2026 (Sunday) + 10 business days:**

| Business Day | Date |
|-------------|------|
| 1 | June 15 (Mon) |
| 2 | June 16 (Tue) |
| 3 | June 17 (Wed) |
| 4 | June 18 (Thu) |
| 5 | June 19 (Fri) |
| [skip weekend] | Jun 20-21 |
| 6 | June 22 (Mon) |
| 7 | June 23 (Tue) |
| 8 | June 24 (Wed) |
| 9 | June 25 (Thu) |
| 10 | June 26 (Fri) |

**10 business days from June 14 = June 26, 2026**

## Applications by Industry

**Legal:** Response deadlines in litigation are usually specified in business days (Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, state court rules). "30 days to respond" in California state court means calendar days; "30 days" in federal court may mean business days — always check the specific rule.

**Shipping:** "Ships within 2 business days" means processing starts immediately but Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays are excluded. An order placed Friday evening typically ships by Tuesday (2 business days from Monday when processing begins).

**HR/Employment:** Notice periods in employment contracts are usually calendar days ("30 days written notice"). Severance pay periods, benefits continuation, and COBRA eligibility windows are typically calendar days.

**Finance:** US stock trades settle T+1 (trade date plus 1 business day). T+1 settlement means a stock sold Monday settles Tuesday; a stock sold Friday settles Monday.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What day is 30 business days from today?**
From June 14, 2026 (Sunday): 30 business days starts counting from June 15 (Monday). 30 business days from June 15 = August 25, 2026 (assuming standard holidays — Juneteenth June 19 reduces count by 1, so July 4 weekend removes 2 calendar days that aren't counted anyway). **Do "business days" always exclude Saturday?**
Conventionally, yes — US business days are Monday through Friday, excluding weekends and federal holidays. However, some industries operate differently: retail businesses operate on weekends; "business days" for banking purposes (ACH transfers) in some contexts may include Saturday; some contracts explicitly define "business days" to include or exclude specific days. Always check the contract or regulatory language for the specific definition being used.

**How do I calculate shipping delivery date?**
Carrier transit time in business days + order processing time. If an item ships in "1–2 business days" and carrier transit is "3–5 business days": total = 4–7 business days from order. If ordered Monday, shipping starts Tuesday–Wednesday; delivery: the following week. Add holiday delays — FedEx and UPS don't deliver on major holidays, and packages in transit may be delayed 1–2 days around Thanksgiving and Christmas.

**How many working days are in a year?**
In the US: 365 calendar days − 104 weekend days (52 Saturdays + 52 Sundays) − 11 federal holidays = **250 working days** (approximately). In leap years: 251 or 252 business days depending on whether Feb 29 is a weekend. Note: 2026 has July 4 on Saturday, so the observed holiday is July 3 (Friday), resulting in 250 business days.

**What is the EXCEL formula for calculating business days?**
NETWORKDAYS function: `=NETWORKDAYS(start_date, end_date, holidays)` returns the number of business days between two dates, including start and end dates. To add business days: `=WORKDAY(start_date, days, holidays)`. Example: `=WORKDAY(DATE(2026,6,14), 30)` returns August 24, 2026 (without holidays). With holiday list in A1:A11: `=WORKDAY(DATE(2026,6,14), 30, A1:A11)`.
