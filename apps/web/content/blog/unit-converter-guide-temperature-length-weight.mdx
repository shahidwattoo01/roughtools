## Metric vs. Imperial: Which Countries Use Which?

The United States, Liberia, and Myanmar are the only countries still primarily using the Imperial system for everyday measurements. Everywhere else uses the metric (SI) system. This creates constant friction for travelers, scientists, cooks, and engineers.

## Temperature Conversion

The three common temperature scales:

- **Celsius (°C):** Water freezes at 0°C, boils at 100°C. Used everywhere except the US.
- **Fahrenheit (°F):** Water freezes at 32°F, boils at 212°F. Used in the United States.
- **Kelvin (K):** Absolute scale used in science. 0 K = absolute zero = -273.15°C.

**Key formulas:**

- °C to °F: (°C × 9/5) + 32
- °F to °C: (°F − 32) × 5/9
- °C to K: °C + 273.15

**Useful references:** 0°C = 32°F (freezing) | 20°C = 68°F (room temp) | 37°C = 98.6°F (body temp) | 100°C = 212°F (boiling)

## Length Conversion

The metric system is based on powers of 10, making conversions straightforward. Imperial units are historical accidents with arbitrary ratios.

| Metric | Imperial Equivalent |
|---|---|

| 1 centimeter | 0.393701 inches |

## Digital Storage Conversion

Digital storage uses two parallel systems: decimal (used by hard drive manufacturers) and binary (used by operating systems). This is why a "1TB" hard drive shows as ~931 GB in Windows.

| Binary (IEC) | Decimal (SI) |
|---|---|
| 1 KiB = 1,024 bytes | 1 KB = 1,000 bytes |
| 1 MiB = 1,048,576 bytes | 1 MB = 1,000,000 bytes |
| 1 GiB = 1,073,741,824 bytes | 1 GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes |
| 1 TiB = 1.0995 × 10¹² bytes | 1 TB = 10¹² bytes |

## Speed Conversion

Common speed conversions:

- 1 mph = 1.60934 km/h
- 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h = 2.23694 mph
- 1 knot = 1.852 km/h = 1.15078 mph
- Mach 1 ≈ 1,235 km/h ≈ 767 mph (at sea level, 15°C)
